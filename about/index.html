About the gallery

The Breakwater Gallery will be an outdoor art space at Port Taranaki, created by the people of Taranaki to highlight the extraordinary talent found in the region. This is a place where local creativity gets the stage it deserves. Where the work of our painters and muralists can be seen, felt, and celebrated by everyone who walks the breakwater.

The vision is simple: to showcase and support local artists, and to give both locals and visitors a destination. Somewhere to slow down, look closely, and see Taranaki through the eyes of the people who create here. As the Gallery grows, it becomes more than a collection of artwork. It will become a landmark. An asset for the region, drawing people to our corner of Aotearoa, giving our creative community a platform to showcase talents, and adding something lasting to Taranaki's identity.

Sponsor a block

Sponsoring a block costs $2,500. Of that, $1,500 goes directly to the artist commissioned to create the work, and $1,000 is donated to the Taranaki Health Foundation for local healthcare projects. Discounted rates are available for multiple block purchases.

As a sponsor, you'll be able to choose which artist you'd like to work with from those registered with the Gallery. However creative control remains with the artist and with the Gallery committee who ensure the artwork is appropriate. This keeps the Gallery a space where artists can create freely, and sponsors can be confident every finished piece belongs on the breakwater.

Founding Patrons

The success of the Breakwater Gallery depends on having great support from the community. Support from businesses and individuals like you, who can see the potential and are willing to take a risk.

Every business, group or individual that joins as a supporter of The Breakwater Gallery in its first year will be recognised as our“Founding Patrons” in perpetuity.

Our founding patrons will be provided a special area of recognition at the start of the Breakwater Gallery and become part of the gallery’s story. They will also be included in marketing promoting the gallery where space allows.

The Breakwater Gallery is an incentive owned by the Taranaki Health Foundation
