The breakwater gallery

Information for patrons

The Breakwater Gallery is a new and exciting arts space for Taranaki. Situated on the Lee Breakwater at Port Taranaki, while being an opportunity for local businesses and individuals to show their support for the region.

See how it works

Supporting local creativity & health

For The Breakwater Gallery to be a success, it needs businesses and people to support it. The gallery receives no external funding from council or government organisations and relies entirely on its patrons.

Your support will make a difference on two levels. Our patrons will be supporting local established artists, as well as up-and-coming artists who want to make an impact locally. In addition to this, the support of patrons will be helping provide

How does it work?

Becoming a patron of the Breakwater Gallery is easy. Every block along Lee Breakwater is available for sponsorship. Each block can be sponsored for $3,000 + GST.

$1,500 of that payment will go directly to the artist for their time, creativity and materials. The remainder will be donated to the Taranaki Health Foundation to provide enhancements to local healthcare.

Payment can be made over two years, with at least 50% required as the initial payment. The proportion that is made as a charitable donation will be eligible donation tax credits from the IRD. Your Patronage will late for three years after which the artwork you sponsor will be replaced.

Patron benefits

As experienced fundraisers, we understand the importance of recognising the generosity of those that make incredible things happen.

Every block that has artwork on it will have a plaque beneath it that recognises the artist and the Patron associated with that specific block.

All patrons will also be recognised on The Breakwater Gallery website. For patrons that have taken advantage of the opportunity to buy multiple blocks, they will receive dedicated promotion on social media and in media releases.

Become a founding patron

We have a limited number of spaces founding patrons. This status is bestowed upon those that take a leap of faith and support one of the first 10 artworks created.

In recognition of our founding patrons support and belief in the project, they will be given recognition in perpetuity at the gallery, for as long as the gallery exists.

Patron FAQs

Do I get to chose the artist I work with?

Artists are encouraged to submit artwork sketches to the gallery. This is then reviewed by the Breakwater Committee and a shortlist of chosen artworks is created. At this point, we will allow Patrons to review the artworks and choose which artworks they want their name against.

Do I get to have any input on the artwork?

In order to get the best work from the artists, their work is created independently from any input from the Gallery or the Patrons. If there are specific artists you would like us to work with, please let us know and we will contact them for submission.

How often is artwork created?

We are working to build up approved artwork designs so that once you come on-board, your artwork can be created straight away. If there isn't anything suitable, we will approach our roster of artists to submit, alongside the general public. There is no set times of year where artwork will be created.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes – you can pay in installments. 50% (or the amount that is paid to the artist) must be paid upfront as this is used to pay the artists. The remainder can be paid over a two-year period.
