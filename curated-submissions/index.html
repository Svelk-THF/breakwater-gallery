The important bits

We are looking for vibrant and exciting artwork to be presented along the Lee Breakwater at Port Taranaki. The space available for each submission is approximately 1.8m x 1.35m.

In recognition of the “Forever” has been part of the Lee Breakwater for some time, we art inviting submissions to tie in with the theme of Forever. How you choose to interpret this is up to you.

And remember, every submission that is chosen and paired with a patron will receive a payment of $1,500*.

3 easy steps

Step 1

Download template

Download our design template, or create your own. Just make sure your design will work within the 1.8m x 1.35m space.

Have a think about the judging criteria, and about how you piece will look outside, both close-up and from a distance.

Step 2

Review your design

Once you have a design, you can see how it will loook in-situ using our Breakwater Gallery Visualiser.

Simply upload a JPG of your design and our visulaiser will show how it looks on a bollard. You can test as many designs as you like - they won't be submitted or seen by anyone until after you have formally submitted using the submission form on this page.

Test your design

Step 3

Submit your design

Once you are happy with your design, come back to this page and upload your design file, ideally as a PDF or JPG and under 5MB.

The submission form also asks for a title for your piece and a rationale behind it. This will help with judging the submission.

Submit your design

Design Preview

Our Breakwater Gallery Visualiser allows you to test how your design will look in-situ before you commit to submitting it.

It's easy to use. Just press the Choose artwork button and find the jpg of your design. Once your image is uploaded, can either upload another image, or reset the image to remove.

Please note: Uploading an image to the visualiser does not mean it is submittted. This is for reference only. To full submit you image, please complete the submission form.
