Ocean's Edge

Heidi Griffin

Website

Inspired by the movement, energy and ever changing mood of the sea, Ocean’s Edge explores the contrast between the untamed power of the ocean and the steadfast strength of the breakwater. A carefully selected limited colour palette combined with simplified lines and bold forms will bring depth, vibrancy and cohesion to the composition. The imagery will celebrate the power and beauty of the ocean while creating a lively, colourful image which will be relevant and suitable for the location and surroundings of The Breakwater Gallery.

The limited palette and simplified imagery isn’t a limitation. It is a deliberate decision. It will help create visual cohesion with variations in tone, intensity and movement while giving the overall composition strength and a pleasing visual engagement.

These visuals are for mockup purposes only. Final artwork is subject to minor changes.
