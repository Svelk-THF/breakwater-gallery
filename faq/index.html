Artist Questions

Getting involved

Who can apply?

Initially we are directly approaching selected artists to submit designs. Once we open up to general submissions, they will be open to anyone. However, the selection process may favour those that have a local connection.

How do I submit, and what do I need to include?

Once public submissions are open, you will be able to download a submission form from our website. This will have a space for your design as well as an opportunity for you to provide a synopsis about your design as well as links to your portfolio if you have one.

Can I apply as a group or collective?

Absolutely. We encourage collaborative work. However the payment will remain at $1,500 which will need to be shared amongst the group.

Can I submit more than one concept?

Yes, there is no limit to how many submissions one artist can make. However multiple submissions will need to be made on individual applications.

Artwork selection

Who chooses the artists, and what criteria is used?

The Breakwater Committee is made up of experts and stakeholders who make the decision on what artwork is approved. Guidelines will be published once public submissions are open.

Do I choose my block, or is it assigned?

The location of your artwork will be assigned to you. This will generally be the next available block in sequence along the breakwater.

Are there limits on what artwork is deemed acceptable?

All artwork must not infringe on the intellectual property rights of any third party, and must not contain material that is offensive, defamatory, political, unlawful or otherwise inappropriate.

Will the patron have a say in my design?

The patron will not have any say in your design. However the Patron does choose which design they would like to sponsor from those that are available.

Payments & rights

Am I paid, and how much? When is payment made?

Each approved submission will be paid $1,500. This will be made directly to the artist. We will endeavour to pay the invoice as soon as possible, but no later than the 20th of the next calendar month.

Do materials come out of my fee, or are they supplied?

All artists materials and expenses are to be paid by the artist. The Breakwater Gallery will cover the cost of preparing the surface and applying a protective coating upon completion of the artwork.

Who owns the artwork and the image rights?

The artist retains the intellectual property rights of their work. By creating work for the Breakwater Gallery, the artist agrees to allow The Breakwater Gallery, Taranaki Health Foundation, Port Taranaki and Creative Taranaki to use footage or imagery of the artwork in promotional materials. All artwork shown in promotional materials will be credited to the artist. This includes use in merchandise to promote the gallery.

Will I be credited on site and online?

Yes. All artists will be credited with a plaque underneath their artwork and on the Breakwater Gallery website.

Practicalities

What paint and sealant should I use for a coastal, salt-exposed surface? Is surface prep done for me?

The surface prep will be done in advance of you starting your artwork. We recommend using a reputable spray paint brand such as Molotow. All artwork will be protected with Graffiti Guard or similar product on completion.

How long do I have to complete the work, and can I paint in stages?

Once your submission has been approved you will be given a two week window in which to complete your work. You can complete the artwork in stages. If the dates given to you don't work, we will work with you to find an alternative window.

How long will the artwork stay up? What happens if it’s damaged?

Artwork will remain in place for three years. As the artist you are welcome to apply touch-ups in this period at your own expense. Any major damage or vandalism will be assessed on an individual basis.

Patron Questions

Patron Benefits

What does sponsoring a block include? How is my support recognised?

First and foremost, by sponsoring a block and becoming a patron you are support the local art community and local healthcare. Your business/name/family will be recognised on a plaque beneath the artwork you are supporting, as well as being recognised on The Breakwater Gallery website. For major donors, there will be additional recognition through media releases and social media. On occasion, we may approach you with the opportunity to be part of videos to promote your involvement.

What are the sponsorship tiers and costs?

The cost of becoming a patron is $3,000. You are welcome to support more than one artwork, with a 5% discount for supporting five or more blocks and a 10% discount for supporting ten.

How long does my sponsorship and recognition last?

You will be recognised as a patron for a minimum of three years, after which we are expecting artworks to be removed and renewed. As an existing patron, you will be given first right of refusal to support the new artwork.

Can I choose the artist and the location?

You will be recognised as a patron for a minimum of three years, after which we are expecting artworks to be removed and renewed. As an existing patron, you will be given first right of refusal to support the new artwork. You will have the opportunity to choose from a selection of artists should you wish. You are also welcome to put forward an artist you would like to support - however they will have to go through the submission process like anyone else. Artworks will be placed sequentially along the gallery space. If you are supporting multiple blocks, you will have the opportunity to have them together or spread out if you wish.

Where the money goes

How much of my sponsorship goes to Taranaki Health Foundation, and how much to the artist?

For every artwork sponsored, $1,500 of your gift will be distributed to the artists for each work of art. The remainder will be donated to the Taranaki Health Foundation.

Is it tax-deductible? Do I get a receipt?

You will be provided with an invoice or receipt for your payment. This will be itemised to show the portion that is going to the artist and the portion that is being donated. You will be able to claim a tax credit on the donation part of the payment.

Other details

Can I sponsor more than one artwork, or as a group of businesses?

Yes, you can sponsor as many artworks as you like. You can also combine with other businesses to support a block.

Can individuals or families sponsor (for example, in memory of someone)?

Yes, we would greatly encourage individuals or families to sponsor, and this can be in memory of someone.

What happens if the artwork I sponsored is damaged or needs repainting?

If something happens to the artwork you sponsored we will assess on an individual basis along with the original artist and ensure repairs are made where necessary.
