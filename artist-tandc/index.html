The Breakwater Gallery

Artists Terms & Conditions

  1. Public submissions can be made from 8 March 2027.
  2. All submissions must be made on the template file downloaded from breakwatergallery.com/submissions.
  3. Submissions will be reviewed by the Breakwater Committee on the last Wednesday of every month. Submissions will be assessed against the published criteria.
  4. If successful, artists will be notified by email or phone.
  5. By submitting artwork, participants confirm that their artwork does not infringe on the intellectual property rights of any third party, and does not contain material that is offensive, defamatory, political, unlawful or otherwise inappropriate.
  6. All artwork must be original work. The artist retains the intellectual property rights of their work. By creating work for the Breakwater Gallery, the artist agrees to allow The Breakwater Gallery, Taranaki Health Foundation, Port Taranaki and Creative Taranaki to use footage or imagery of the artwork in promotional materials. All artwork shown in promotional materials will be credited to the artist. This includes use in merchandise to promote the gallery.
  7. The final design must not be created or generated in any substantial way or assisted by artificial intelligence tools. The Breakwater Committee may require applicants to show preliminary sketches, drafts, or similar designs to show the development process.
  8. The created artwork must accurately reflect the approved submission. Any substantial changes must be approved by the Breakwater Committee in advance.
  9. The Breakwater Gallery reserves the right to exclude or remove any artwork, should these conditions be broken.
The Breakwater Gallery is an incentive owned by the Taranaki Health Foundation. All prices on this site are ex. GST.
