The breakwater gallery

Information for artists

The Breakwater Gallery is a new and exciting arts space for Taranaki. Situated on the Lee Breakwater at Port Taranaki, the Gallery will showcase the region’s best outdoor artists, as well as being an opportunity for up-and-coming artists to display their talent.

What you can expect

We are looking for talented artists to create a premium destination for people in Taranaki. We want to turn the Lee Breakwater from a dull and grey industrial space to a vibrant destination that shows the amazing talent this region has.

The gallery will be funded by patrons who choose an artist or artwork that they want to support. With their generous contribution, any artist that creates artwork for the gallery will be paid $1,500 for each approved submission, as well as a $1,500 donation per artwork being made to the Taranaki Health Foundation.

Approved artists will also be given a profile on this website and be promoted in media where possible.
