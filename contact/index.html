Contact us

The Breakwater Gallery exists for Taranaki. It is there to support the local art community, provide enhancements for local healthcare, and provide everyone a space to go and find some peace.

Whether you are an artist who would like to know more about a submission, a potential patron or would just like to leave a message for us, please do so using the form below. We would love to hear from you!

The Breakwater Gallery is an incentive owned by the Taranaki Health Foundation. All prices on this site are ex. GST.
